About the Magaji Project

A transparent partnership development in Bamenda.

Magaji is a collaborative land development project structured through a proven crowdfunding model, bringing buyers together to secure high-value property at substantial bulk discounts.

By pooling buyer demand, we acquire and title large land tracts directly, eliminating unnecessary intermediary markups. Because this community model relies on committed partners, we screen all inquiries through a short qualification process before scheduling on-site inspections.

Anti-Scam Protection

How to Verify This Land Is 100% Genuine

Land fraud is unfortunately common. That is why RADSSEKAV insists that every buyer completes thorough verification before exchanging any money.

1

See the Original Title

We present the physical, original land title document for your inspection — never a photocopy or unverifiable draft.

2

Walk the Plot

Visit Magaji in person. Walk the actual boundaries, inspect the beacon pillars, and examine the topography firsthand.

3

Check at Land Registry

You or your attorney can cross-reference the cadastral survey number directly with the regional land registry authorities.

4

Pay Only When Satisfied

Zero advance pressure. You execute a legally binding agreement and pay only after you and your advisors are completely satisfied.
