How to check this plot is real

Land scams are common. So we ask you to verify before any payment.

  1. See the original title.We show you the original document, not a photocopy.
  2. Walk the plot.Visit the land and see the boundaries yourself.
  3. Check at the land registry.You or your lawyer can confirm the title with the authorities.
  4. Pay only after you are satisfied.Ask for a written agreement and an official receipt.
